Selected Dainas

The Latvian dainas are an oral tradition of over 200,000 folk songs. Collected by Krišjānis Barons in the late 19th century, they encode a complete pre-Christian cosmology of sun, earth, and cyclical time.

Position

Re-centering pre-Christian Baltic cosmology through disciplined modern design. Replacing abstract consumption with land-rooted objects.

Each artisan carries documented lineage: who they are, the region they work in, the daina that accompanies their craft, and the solar season in which their work is offered.

This is not a shop. It is a cultural export layer. A disciplined aesthetic system. A marketplace functioning as modern solar canon.
